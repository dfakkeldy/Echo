Release preparation · November 15, 2026

Preparing Echo 1.1

A continuous reader, clearer controls, and more ways to narrate and capture.

This page describes the Nightly candidate. It does not announce Apple approval or availability.

What changed since 1.0

  • A continuous reader, simpler iPhone controls and clearer chapter labels.
  • Visual Listening keeps figures visible while nearby text scrolls.
  • PDF read-along sidecars can supply narration text and exact word boxes.
  • Optional Phonon 31M narration on supported hardware. Kokoro remains the default.
  • Watch Inbox dictates to a configured HTTPS webhook through iPhone, with optional book context and queued-delivery recovery.
  • The iPhone and iPad Books shelf shows listening history and groups related editions.
  • Fixes for bookmark persistence, library scans, reader loading and EPUB pronunciation handling.

The Siri and Watch position fix is also in the 1.0.1 patch source. Its availability in the store needs confirmation.

Before you install

iPhone and iPad require iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. Watch requires watchOS 11 or later and is a companion to the iPhone player. Mac requires macOS 15 or later and Apple Silicon.

Full private-iCloud sync of ordinary cards, decks, bookmarks and playback is unfinished. Sharing an audio folder does not transfer your study database. Optional AI providers, Audiobookshelf, article sync and Watch Inbox use network services.

On iPhone and iPad, Echo Pro is a one-time unlock for the one-chapter-per-book free narration limit and the 20-card limit. Mac currently has no Pro purchase interface and does not apply those limits.

Read the manual, privacy policy and beta guide for details.

The source-backed change inventory records the baseline and linked changes. The older April v1.1 tag is unrelated to this candidate.
