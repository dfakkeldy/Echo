Know what goes where

One project.
Different strengths.

The manual follows current Nightly source. Your TestFlight build may be older; its notes tell you what changed.

iPhone & iPad iOS 18+
Playback, reading, narration, study, and detailed Insights. Echo Pro is a one-time unlock for the phone's additional limits and features.
Apple Watch watchOS 11+
A paired-phone remote, capture, review, and timers. Circular and rectangular Watch widgets keep progress close.
Mac macOS 15+
A Library and reader workspace, notes, daily review, batch narration, Audiobookshelf, and export. Some phone study features differ.

Your library, your choices

Keep your books.
Keep your work.

Local playback, narration inference, and alignment run on your device. Optional connections — model downloads, external AI, Audiobookshelf, article sync, or your own Watch Inbox — have their own data handling.

Source files in iCloud Drive and Audiobookshelf progress are useful. They are separate from full iCloud study sync, which remains unfinished. The sync guide explains the difference.

Echo is open source under GPL-3.0. Export your study notes and chaptered audio, and read the code when you want to know how it works.

Built around an interrupted day

It started on a mail route.

Dan wanted to loop a difficult chapter, save a thought, and pick up the thread after the next interruption. Echo grew out of using it every day, with Dan directing AI coding agents to build and refine the app.

Designed around neurodivergent attention. Useful wherever your listening happens.
