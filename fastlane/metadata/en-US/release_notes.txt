Echo 1.1 brings a continuous reader, clearer chapter labels and simpler iPhone controls.

- Keep figures visible while reading in Visual Listening.
- Use PDF read-along files for authored narration text and precise word highlighting.
- Try optional Phonon 31M English narration on supported hardware. Kokoro remains the default.
- Dictate to a configured HTTPS webhook with Watch Inbox, with optional book context and retries for messages waiting to send.
- Find books you have started listening to on the iPhone and iPad Books shelf, with related editions grouped together.
- Improved bookmark persistence, library scans, EPUB pronunciation handling and reader loading.
