Listen to your own audiobooks, read alongside them, and save passages to study later. Echo plays DRM-free audio and can narrate EPUB, PDF, Markdown and plain text documents on your device.

LISTEN AND READ
- Import MP3, M4A and M4B audiobooks, including books split across files.
- Adjust playback speed, loop chapters or bookmarks, set a sleep timer, and use Smart Rewind to regain context after an interruption.
- Add EPUB or PDF companion text. Search, align text with audio, and return to useful passages. Alignment quality depends on the recording and edition.
- Follow a continuous reader. Visual Listening keeps figures visible while nearby text scrolls.
- Use PDF read-along companion files, when supplied with a document, for authored narration text and precise word highlighting.

NARRATE YOUR DOCUMENTS
- Choose a voice and narrate text with Kokoro on-device after downloading its model. Add pronunciation corrections for names and technical terms.
- Try the optional Phonon 31M English narration engine on supported hardware. It has its own model download and paragraph-level read-along; exact word timing and pronunciation corrections remain Kokoro features.
- Export narrated or imported audio as a chaptered M4B file.

CAPTURE AND STUDY
- Save bookmarks with notes, photos or voice memos.
- Mark a passage, revisit it in Card Inbox, and create or edit flashcards.
- Review cards with spaced repetition and use chapter Study Plans.
- Track listening and study activity on iPhone and iPad. Export study notes for use elsewhere.

CONNECT WHEN YOU CHOOSE
- Browse and download from your Audiobookshelf server and exchange listening progress.
- Control the iPhone player and review cards from Apple Watch.
- Configure Watch Inbox to dictate messages to your own HTTPS webhook through iPhone, optionally including the current book and position.
- Mac offers playback, reading, narration, Card Inbox, Daily Review and batch document narration. Features differ by platform. Sharing source files does not sync the full study database.

MADE FOR YOUR READING PREFERENCES
Choose Lexend or OpenDyslexic, adjust text size, and use VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support.

FREE CORE, OPTIONAL PRO
Core import, playback and reading are free. On iPhone and iPad, free narration covers one chapter per book and flashcards are limited to 20. Echo Pro is a one-time unlock for those limits. Mac currently has no Pro purchase interface and does not apply those phone limits.

Echo is open-source under GPL-3.0. Local playback, narration inference and alignment process files on-device. Model downloads and optional AI providers, Audiobookshelf, article sync and Watch Inbox use network services. External AI sends source text to the provider you select. Read the privacy policy for details. No Echo account is required for core playback.
