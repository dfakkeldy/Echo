Core book import, local playback, reading, and voice synthesis run on-device without an Echo account. AI-provider and Audiobookshelf features, iCloud sync, community alignment discovery and sharing, and model downloads use network services. iPhone/iPad review path: add any DRM-free EPUB from Files, then tap Narrate — Echo synthesizes narration on-device after a one-time voice-model download. Audiobook playback: add a DRM-free audiobook (m4b or mp3 folder) the same way. On iPhone/iPad, Echo Pro is an optional one-time non-consumable in-app purchase (com.echo.pro.unlock), not a subscription. Core import, playback, and reading are free. On iPhone/iPad without Pro, narration covers one chapter per book and flashcards are capped at 20; transcript overlays, insights, study export, and Audiobookshelf offline sync are part of Pro. On iPhone/iPad, Purchase and Restore Purchases are on the Echo Pro screen in Settings and on every Pro prompt. The current Mac candidate has no Echo Pro purchase/restore UI and does not apply the iPhone/iPad narration or flashcard caps. For Mac review, use Batch > Narrate Documents… for narration and skip the iPhone/iPad purchase/restore steps.
