Echo runs entirely on-device and requires no login or account. Quickest review path: add any DRM-free EPUB from Files, then tap Narrate — Echo synthesizes narration on-device after a one-time voice-model download. Audiobook playback: add a DRM-free audiobook (m4b or mp3 folder) the same way. This build ships with every feature unlocked: Echo Pro is not purchasable yet, there is no paywall, and no in-app purchase is present. Nothing relevant to review requires a purchase.
