Welcome to the Echo beta! Echo is an audiobook STUDY player: it lines audio up with the book text word by word, narrates text-only books entirely on-device, and turns what you hear into notes and flashcards. Core book import, local playback, reading, and voice synthesis run on-device without an Echo account. AI-provider and Audiobookshelf features, iCloud sync, community alignment discovery and sharing, and model downloads use network services.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES
• Add a DRM-free EPUB and tap Narrate. Echo synthesizes the voice on-device — the first run downloads the voice model once, so note how long the first spoken words take on your device.
• Or add an audiobook (m4b, or a folder of mp3s) with its matching EPUB and run Auto-Align Chapters, then watch the text follow the narrator as it plays.

WHAT TO FOCUS ON

1) NARRATION (on-device text-to-speech)
• Do numbers, prices, and abbreviations read out naturally? Report the exact sentence for anything mispronounced — those reports drive real fixes.
• Read along while it narrates: words should light up in sync. Tap a paragraph — the voice should jump there.
• Switch narrator voice and re-narrate a chapter.

2) LOOK & FEEL — cover theming (new)
• In Settings, try the Cover appearance mode: the whole app takes its tone from the playing book's cover. Try the Vivid Cover Accent toggle too.
• If any book's cover makes the app ugly or hard to read, screenshot it — real covers are the test.

3) LIBRARY
• Import several books, including the same book as both EPUB and audiobook: you should get ONE card per book, with the right cover.

4) PDF COMPANION
• Add a PDF (textbook, paper): continuous scroll and pinch-zoom should stay smooth; with matching audio, tap-to-seek and read-along should track.

5) STUDY & EXPORT
• Create flashcards from what you're hearing, then export your study notes as Markdown — do counts and formatting hold up?
• Export an audiobook as .m4b — do chapters and cover art survive in another player?

6) APPLE WATCH
• Play from the wrist. The crown adjusts volume, and progress and cover should stay in sync when the watch app reopens mid-playback.

ECHO PRO IN THIS BETA
This build includes the Echo Pro paywall. Echo Pro is a one-time unlock, never a subscription. Free use covers import, playback, reading, one narrated chapter per book, and up to 20 flashcards. TestFlight purchases go through Apple's sandbox, so you aren't charged. Try buying and restoring Echo Pro (Settings > Echo Pro), and report any price that won't load, a stuck pending purchase, a restore that doesn't unlock, or a feature that stays locked after purchase.

REPORTING
Use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback (a screenshot attaches automatically). For narration or alignment bugs, include the book title, narrator, and where the EPUB came from. Books that behave badly are gold — we want them.
